says an entry, **if any**, cannot be below 20% of principal. Qwen instead asserted
that entry is always mandatory and computed 20% of 5000.00 as 1000.00 without a
financial tool. Correct arithmetic does not make that authorized.
The cash follow-up claimed payment without discount was unsupported, although
`discount.md` says to present it without discount when the engine returns none.
No offer engine ran in those natural-language turns.

The action guard does not validate already-streamed prose. Backend rejection
does not make previously displayed text safe or faithful.
